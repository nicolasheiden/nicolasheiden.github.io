### My Roles
- Terminal Designer/Programmer
- Level Select Designer/Programmer
- Level Designer

Shifting Order is a 2D puzzle-platformer developed in Unity, starring a red panda who must manipulate the world through a Terminal to solve puzzles and reach the end of each level.

My primary responsibility was developing and refining the Terminal interaction system, the core mechanic around which the gameplay is built. My focus was on making the interactions intuitive, responsive, and satisfying to use, ensuring that manipulating the world felt natural and engaging for the player.

I also designed and implemented the Level Select system, inspired by an elevator button panel with multiple pages of levels. The system tracks the player's completed levels and automatically selects the next level upon completion, creating a smooth progression through the game.

To bring the Level Select to life, I made extensive use of tweening to add fluid animations and responsive visual feedback. This helped give the interface a polished feel while reinforcing the elevator-inspired design.

Through Shifting Order, I gained experience in designing core gameplay interactions, implementing progression systems, and using animation to improve the overall feel and responsiveness of a game's user interface.

<h3 id="bullet-row"><a class="project-title-link" href="https://yrgo.itch.io/bullet-row" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bullet Row</a></h3>
<figure class="project-showcase">

### My Roles
- Shop Designer/Programmer
- Item Designer/Programmer
- Gambling Feature Designer/Programmer
- Gun Programmer

Bullet Row is a roguelike aim trainer developed entirely in Unreal Engine 5 using Blueprints. Set in a robotic Wild West, the game challenges players to visit a shooting gallery, take down robotic targets, and earn points in the form of Bounty and currency known as Bartender Bucks. These rewards allow players to purchase upgrades and keep pushing forward.

My primary responsibility was developing the shop system, allowing players to shoot items to add them to their inventory and sell them for currency. By utilizing Unreal Engine's interfaces and event system, I created a functional and flexible system that integrated smoothly with the game's mechanics.

Beyond the shop system, I designed many of the game's items, ranging from straightforward upgrades to more complex and unconventional effects. One of my favorite contributions was designing the slot machine, a feature that lets players gamble their Bartender Bucks for a chance to win more money. Originally put together over a single weekend, it quickly became a favorite among our playtesters.

I also contributed to the development of the Gun Blueprint, which serves as the foundation for the player's primary interaction with the game.

Through Bullet Row, I gained hands-on experience in gameplay systems, Blueprint-based development, and item design, while contributing features that helped shape the game's identity and player experience.

<h3 id="poggle-ascension">Poggle Ascension</h3>
<div class="project-gallery poggle-gallery">
	<figure>
		<img src="/Images/bingustitle.png" alt="Bingus title screen from Poggle Ascension">

	<figure>
		<img src="/Gifs/PA_Gameplay.gif" alt="Poggle Ascension gameplay">

Poggle Ascension is a small, solo-developed mobile game created in Unity. Inspired by hearing about Peglin but never having played it, I set out to recreate the core concept based solely on what I knew about the game. I also designed and made all the art in the game using Asesprite.

The result is a short roguelike inspired by pachinko, where players launch Orbs onto a peg-filled board to earn enough points to progress to the next level. Between levels, players can purchase new Orbs and rearrange their firing order, adding a layer of strategy and progression to each run.

The project was an opportunity to explore a new gameplay concept independently while challenging myself to recreate a game experience from secondhand knowledge.
